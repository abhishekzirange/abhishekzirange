Enterprise Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) with Multi-Agent AI

Business Problem

Deep Learning Monocular Depth Estimation (ResNet-50)

Business Problem

Estimating accurate 3D scene depth without deploying expensive LiDAR or stereo sensors, enabling spatial analytics from single standard camera feeds.

Technical Architecture

Built an encoder-decoder network with a ResNet-50 backbone utilizing residual identity gradient flow ($\mathbf{I}$). Trained with reverse Huber (BerHu) loss to balance small residual gradients against heavy depth boundary edges.

Verified Impact

Achieved sharp depth boundary separation and low root-mean-square error (RMSE), powering downstream spatial analytics from monocular inputs.

Distributed Systems & Document AI Celery Chords · 99.9% Uptime

Low-Code OCR Training & Asynchronous Task Engine

Business Problem

Enterprises needed custom model extraction for non-standard documents. Heavy 100-page PDF uploads caused head-of-line blocking in default queues, starving real-time receipt processing.

Technical Architecture

Decomposed multi-page documents via Celery Chords (`chord(group(ocr_page))(aggregate)`). Configured `worker_prefetch_multiplier = 1` for fair queueing, Redis distributed locks (`SETNX`), and `worker_max_tasks_per_child = 100` to prevent C-level OpenCV memory leaks.

Verified Impact

Enabled non-technical operators to train custom extraction models in minutes; eliminated worker crashes and achieved 99.9% pipeline uptime across AWS S3 and GCP.

Celery Chords Redis Idempotency Docker MultiOutputRegressor AWS S3 / GCP

AI Signature Verification & One-Shot Metric Learning

Business Problem

Banking and enterprise KYC workflows required verifying customer signatures against 1 or 2 reference samples, with thousands of new customers onboarding daily.

Technical Architecture

Engineered an open-set one-shot verification pipeline: YOLOv4 localized signature bounding boxes; twin Siamese Networks trained with Contrastive & Triplet Loss projected signatures into a 128-D Euclidean metric space.

Verified Impact

Verified new customer signatures in real time with zero model retraining, significantly reducing verification fraud and accelerating onboarding approval times.
